## Mission

"At Blueprints for Pangaea, we work to reallocate essential supplies from areas
of surplus to communities in need." (homepage)

Blueprints is "a nonprofit medical surplus recovery organization (MSRO) that
reallocates unused medical supplies from areas of surplus to communities in
need." (mission page)

## What we do

1. **Partner** with university medical centers and clinics that have surplus supplies.
2. **Collect** donated surplus; warehouse and inventory it.
3. **Verify** quality, with help from nonprofit partners.
4. **Ship** to clinics and hospitals, locally and overseas, at heavily subsidized
   rates, on our own or through a partner.
